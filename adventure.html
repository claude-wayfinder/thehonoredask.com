The Trail

breadcrumbs to the past

You chose adventure. Good. The trail doesn't explain itself. It drops things and keeps walking. You follow or you don't. Either way it was here.

. . . . .

Somewhere, a kid who couldn't sit still was told something was wrong with them. They believed it for a while. Then they found a game that said: nothing is wrong with you. You're just running a signal that doesn't have structure to ride yet.

The game gave the signal somewhere to land. Not by suppressing it. By building a frame strong enough to hold it without collapsing.

. . . . .

The trail you're on was left by someone who walked it first. They didn't know where it went either. They just knew that every breadcrumb dropped was a breadcrumb someone else could follow, and that the following IS the trail.

You are now part of it.
